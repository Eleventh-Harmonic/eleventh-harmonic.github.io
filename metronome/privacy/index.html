Metronome Eleven Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy explains how the Metronome Eleven app for Android handles information. Metronome Eleven is published by Eleventh Harmonic.

Privacy at a glance

  • We run no servers for the app. The app has no accounts, ads or tracking.
  • Your settings are stored on your device, and in your Android backup if you use one.
  • Google Play handles purchases. We do not receive your payment card or bank account details.
  • Google’s billing library, which the app needs for purchases, sends Google technical diagnostics.
  • If you email us, we use your message to answer you and to improve the app.

Google is responsible for its own processing, described in the sections on purchases, diagnostics and backups.

What the app does not do

We run no servers for the app, and the app does not send information to us. The app has no accounts or sign-in and does not use the advertising ID. It does not ask for permission to access your location, contacts, microphone, camera, photos or files.

We have not included any advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking software in the app. Google Play Billing Library, which the app needs for purchases, sends Google its own diagnostics, described in Diagnostics sent by Google Play Billing Library. Apart from that library, the app does not connect to the internet itself. Links, sharing and email hand over to other apps on your device.

Information stored on your device

To remember your choices, the app stores on your device:

  • your settings: tempo, time signature and accents, tempo presets, click sound, skin and display options;
  • whether the premium unlock is active.

The app does not send this information to us. Android keeps it in the app’s private storage, separate from other apps.

If backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this information in your device backup and restore it on a new device. Google stores these backups in your Google Account under its own privacy policy. We do not have access to your backups.

Purchases through Google Play

The app offers one optional in-app purchase: an unlock for the premium skins. Google Play handles the purchase and the payment under the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Google collects your payment details directly. The app does not receive them, and Google does not give them to us.

The app uses Google Play Billing Library, made by Google, to talk to Google Play. When the app starts, it asks Google Play for the price of the unlock and whether your Google Account already owns it, for example after you reinstall the app. When you choose to buy, it starts the purchase and then confirms the completed purchase to Google Play.

Google shares limited order information with us in the Google Play Console, for example the order number, product, price, date, status and the buyer’s country or region. Google also lets us find an order by the buyer’s email address, for example when you ask us for help with a purchase. We use this information to manage orders and refunds, to help you with purchases, and to meet our tax and accounting obligations. Our legal bases under the GDPR are our legitimate interest in managing purchases and helping you (Article 6(1)(f)) and our legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)).

Diagnostics sent by Google Play Billing Library

When the app uses Google Play Billing Library, including when the app starts, the library may send Google technical information about its own operation, such as whether billing requests succeed or fail and problems with the connection to Google Play. Google’s software adds basic device and app details to this information: Android version, device model and manufacturer, system build, language and country setting, time zone, network type, mobile network operator code and the app’s version number.

This information goes directly to Google over an encrypted connection, not to us. Google says it uses it to improve the library and to provide better support for errors. Google decides how it uses this information, under the Google Privacy Policy.

Android permissions

  • Foreground service for media playback: keeps the metronome playing when you leave the app, with a notification that has a Stop button.
  • Google Play billing: needed for the in-app purchase.
  • Internet and network state: added by Google Play Billing Library so it can send its diagnostics to Google.

Links, sharing and feedback

These features hand over to another app on your device, and only when you tap them:

  • Rate on Google Play and the links on the About page open the Google Play Store app or your web browser.
  • Share app opens Android’s share menu with a short text and the app’s Google Play link. You choose the app to share with. The app does not store or send anything about what you share.
  • Send feedback opens a draft email to us in your email app. The draft includes the app version, a technical version string of your system and the app’s language, to help us find problems. You can edit or delete these details. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself.

The privacy policies of those apps and services apply to what you do in them.

Emails you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if your email account shows it, your message and anything you include, such as screenshots or the details in the feedback draft. Emailing us is optional. You do not need to give us any personal information to use the app.

We use this information to answer you, to help you with a purchase, and to fix problems and improve the app. Our legal basis under the GDPR is our legitimate interest in answering you and improving the app (Article 6(1)(f)).

We receive email with Gmail, a Google service. Google stores the messages on its servers, which may be in other countries, including the United States. Google says it relies on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and on standard contractual clauses for such transfers; see Google’s data transfer frameworks.

Who receives information

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not use or share it for advertising. We do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

  • Google receives information as the provider of Google Play (purchases and order information), Google Play Billing Library (diagnostics) and Gmail (emails you send us). For Google Play and the billing library, Google decides how it uses the information, under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Authorities or others may receive information if the law requires us to disclose it.

We do not give your information to anyone else.

Security

Android keeps the information the app stores private to the app. Google Play Billing Library sends its diagnostics over an encrypted connection. We take reasonable steps to protect the emails we receive, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Retention and deletion

  • Settings on your device stay until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes them from your device. A copy in your device backup follows your Google Account backup settings.
  • Your purchase is linked to your Google Account, so you can restore it after you reinstall the app. Uninstalling the app does not remove it. Google keeps purchase records under its own policies.
  • Order information stays in the Google Play Console under Google’s retention rules. If we keep copies for tax or accounting, we keep them only as long as those laws require.
  • Billing Library diagnostics are kept by Google under its own policies.
  • Emails are kept as long as we need them to answer you and to follow up on the problem you reported. We delete them when you ask, unless the law requires us to keep them.

To delete the app’s data from your device, uninstall the app, or open your device settings, go to Apps, then Metronome Eleven, then Storage, and clear the storage. To have your emails deleted, write to us.

Children

Metronome Eleven is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not collect personal information through the app from anyone, including children. Google processes information for purchases and billing diagnostics as described above. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws, you have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • have it corrected or deleted;
  • restrict or object to its use;
  • receive it in a portable format.

The app does not collect or transmit personal information to us during normal use. If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to share. If you purchase the premium unlock, we can access limited order information through Google Play Console. We do not maintain our own customer database. To use your rights, email us. We normally answer within one month.

You can also complain to a data protection authority: in the Netherlands, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, or the authority in the country where you live or work.

Google is responsible for the information it processes for Google Play, purchases, backups and billing diagnostics. You can manage much of it in your Google Account.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, the services it uses or the law changes. We will post every update on this page and change the “Last updated” date. If a change is significant, we will also mention it in the app’s release notes.
